About Fountain

Indigenous. Registered. On the water since 2009.

Fountain Shipping Services Limited has worked Nigeria's ports and terminals since 2009. We handle ship agency, logistics and marine services for vessel owners and oil traders operating in West Africa's territorial waters, both onshore and offshore.

We're registered with the government agencies that oversee shipping in Nigeria, and our offices sit where the work happens: Port Harcourt, Bonny Island, Lagos, Warri and Calabar.

Our occupational health and safety system is certified to ISO 45001:2018. That's not a certificate on the wall. It's how we run vessel calls, chandelling runs and cargo transfers, day to day.

Mission Deliver shipping services that meet the standard the marine industry expects, every time.
Vision Become the shipping company West Africa turns to first, for how the work gets done as much as what gets delivered.
Values
